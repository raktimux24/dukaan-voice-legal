# Stock and buying: design review (2026-11-12)

Scope: Products, product form/detail, bulk edit, barcodes, product import, Buy list, Suppliers, Purchase orders, Supplier invoices.
Sources: screenshots 04–09 (1440px, top of page only) and code under `app/components/shop/screens`, `app/components/shop`, `app/lib/shop/purchase-orders/view.tsx`, `app/shop/shop.css`. Findings that come only from reading code are marked "(from code)".

## 1. Verdict

The individual screens look calm and high-end: they share a typographic system, use generous spacing and show clear count tiles. The buying process, though, is four separate tools that don't hand off well. Buy list, Purchase orders, Supplier invoices and Suppliers each have their own vocabulary, entry points and confirmation rituals. Two of them (Purchase orders and Supplier invoices) don't appear in the sidebar at all. The worst problems are three defaults. A new product's low-stock alert starts at 0, so stock alerts are off. Recording a supplier invoice adds no stock unless you change a dropdown on every line. Saving an invoice requires ticking a GST-law checkbox written for an accountant. The going from "I'm running low" to "goods on the shelf and bill recorded" takes about 30 clicks, 3 attestation checkboxes and 4 pages. Fix the defaults, merge the restock process into one guided path, and replace the legal copy with plain language, and this area will feel premium as well as look it.

## 2. Top 5 changes

1. **Make restocking one guided path.** Add a "Restock" group to the sidebar: Buy list, Orders, Deliveries & bills. Let users multi-select buy-list items and create one order per supplier from them. When an order is received, offer "Record the bill now" on the same screen.
2. **Fix the three bad defaults.** Suggest a low-stock level for new products instead of 0. Default the invoice stock action to "Add to stock" when no order is linked. Drop the blocking "ordinary domestic forward-charge" checkbox; detect the unusual cases instead.
3. **Cut the attestation checkboxes.** Replace "I checked the supplier, quantities…", "I checked accepted quantities…" and "I have reviewed the invoice…" with a single review summary and one primary button. Keep a checkbox only where an action really can't be undone.
4. **Let users add the whole shortfall at once.** On the Products "Low stock" view and on Home alerts, add "Add all N to buy list". On the buy list, add "Select → Order from supplier". Restocking 15 items should take 3 clicks, not 75.
5. **Make import forgiving.** Let users import the valid rows and skip the rest in one step ("Import 186, skip 14 with problems"). Allow create and update in the same file. Show the review as a compact table, not one card per row.

## 3. Flow walkthrough

### A. Add a first product (owner, empty shop)
1. Products (sidebar) shows the empty state: "No products in this view." with "Reset filters" and "Add product" (`products.tsx:478-494`, from code). There's no "Import from Excel" here, and "Reset filters" makes no sense on a brand-new shop.
2. Click "+ Add" (SAW, 04; the label is just "Add"). The form opens with Category already set to "Mobiles & Tablets · Smartphones" (SAW, 05). A user who doesn't notice files every product under the wrong category.
3. The section index "01 Details / 02 Prices & GST / 03 Stock / 04 Product photos" looks like a stepper but is just anchor links (`product-form.tsx:296-298`, from code). Users will expect a Next button that doesn't exist. A fifth card (Short code · Buy in packs) isn't in the index.
4. The "No barcode? Generate one when saving" checkbox comes *before* the Barcode field (SAW, 05).
5. Prices & GST: buying price and buying discount come before selling price, followed by GST tax fields and an MRP toggle (from code). For a shopkeeper, selling price is the first thing that matters.
6. Stock: "Low-stock alert at" defaults to **0** (`product-form.tsx:57`, from code). The product will never show as low stock or reach the buy list.
7. Save stays dimmed until there's a name. The only explanation is tiny centred text under the footer: "Product name is required" (SAW, 05).
8. After saving, the product page opens with "Stock history" (a Premium-gated snapshot card) above the product summary and stock actions (`product-detail.tsx:303-330`, from code).
**About 3 clicks and 2 required fields at minimum, but the defaults quietly produce a badly set-up product.**

### B. Import 200 products from Excel
1. Products → "Product tools +" (small text link at the top right of the catalogue card; SAW, 04) → "Import products". Import is buried two levels deep.
2. Upload step: one button, no drag-and-drop zone; the file input is visually hidden (`inventory-import.tsx:42-43`, from code). "Download template" is offered at the same weight as the main action.
3. Mapping step: sheet, **Import mode (Add new *or* Update existing)**, a default Category/Sub-category for the whole file, Opening stock date, then column mapping (`inventory-import.tsx:53-59`, from code). A file that mixes new and existing items needs two passes.
4. Review step: each row is a full Card, 50 at a time (`:67-74`). Save stays disabled while *any* row has an error (`:77`, `counts.errors>0`). With 14 bad rows the user clicks "Skip" 14 times, or fixes the file and starts over.
5. Tick "Save these reviewed changes? Prices and new-product stock will be applied in recoverable batches." → Save.
6. The help text says "Only the owner can import stock." (`imports.stock_help`). A manager's import silently arrives without stock, and nothing at the moment of import says so.
**A clean file takes about 7 clicks. A realistic messy file can take 25+, or a full restart.**

### C. Low stock → buy list → purchase order → receive → supplier bill
1. **Notice:** Products → "Low stock 3" tile (SAW, 04) → open a product → "Add to buy list" (`product-detail.tsx:440-472`, from code). The quantity is set silently to the alert level, or 1. There's no bulk "add all low stock". Repeat for each product: about 3 clicks × N.
   Alternative: Buy list → "Add item +" → search → pick → quantity → Add (5 actions per item).
2. **Plan:** Buy list (SAW, 06). Each row has "Add Batch →", "Order" and "Delete". There's no multi-select, so "Order" starts a purchase order for **one** item (`buy-list.tsx:314-326`). The user then has to re-add the other items inside the PO picker.
3. **Order:** PO compose. Step 1: choose supplier (+ date, notes) → Continue. Step 2: tap buy-list items or products in a long picker → edit quantity and estimated price → Continue. Step 3: Review → **Save draft**. That lands on order detail, Details tab. Switch to the **Actions** tab → tick "I checked the supplier, quantities, units and estimated prices." → **Issue order** → **Share order**. On desktop, Share downloads `purchase-order.txt` (`purchase-orders.tsx:104-116`). About 9 clicks and 1 attestation.
4. **Receive:** There's no sidebar link to Purchase orders (SAW: Stock group = Products, Buy list, Suppliers). Path is Home or Buy list → "Purchase orders ↗" → open order → "Receive delivery" → per line: accepted quantity, unit cost, batch, expiry, rejected → tick "I checked accepted quantities…" → "Confirm delivery receipt". About 6 clicks, 1 attestation and a lot of typing.
5. **Bill:** History → "Record / open supplier invoice" → invoice form opens at step 2. Enter invoice number, date, "Purchase price entry", discount, **Original invoice total (required)** and "Evidence reference", review the shop recipient address, tick the 40-word "ordinary domestic forward-charge…" checkbox → Continue. Step 3: review GST fields per line → tick "I have reviewed the invoice, tax details and stock actions." → "Record supplier invoice". About 5 clicks, 2 attestations and a total that must match.
**End to end: about 30 clicks, 4 attestation checkboxes and 4 pages with different headers. Stock arrival goes by four names: "Add Batch", "Receive stock", "Receive delivery" and "Receive new stock".**

**Where users get lost:**
- **No sidebar entry for POs or invoices.** Supplier invoices are reached from Settings, a supplier record, or the PO. Settings links to `/shop/purchases`, which redirects to the *new invoice form* (`app/shop/purchases/page.tsx:11`), so the list of recorded invoices is never shown.
- **The PO page's back link goes to Home,** not to the Buy list the user came from (`purchase-orders.tsx:77`).
- **The two supplier pages contradict each other.** Suppliers lists "saved suppliers" and separate "Stock supplier history" names, and each opens a different detail page (`/suppliers/records/[id]` vs `/suppliers/[name]`). For a shopkeeper, "Metro Distributors" is one supplier.
- **Two ways to receive stock.** Stock can arrive through product page → "Receive stock" *or* invoice → "Receive new stock" *or* PO → "Receive delivery". Nothing explains which to use, or that using two of them double-counts.

## 5. Not checked

- **Not seen live:** anything below the fold or behind interaction (dialogs, mapping and review screens, PO detail tabs, receipt form, invoice steps 2–3, product detail, bulk edit, barcode screens). These were judged from code only.
- **Not tested:** real loading timing, error responses, offline behaviour and save-recovery flows.
- **Not traced:** whether receiving a PO marks linked buy-list items as "Stocked".
- **Not verified:** the actual rendered translations in Indian languages, or text overflow with them.
- **Not checked:** keyboard-only navigation and screen-reader output in a real browser, and measured colour contrast (only token values were inspected).
- **Not checked:** mobile and tablet layouts beyond reading the media queries.
- **Not checked:** Premium-gated states (Suggestions tab, stock history) with a Premium vs non-Premium account.
- **Not reviewed:** supplier import (`party-import.tsx`) beyond confirming it reuses the product-import pattern (same findings S22–S25 likely apply).
- **Not reviewed:** purchase detail (payments, supplier credit, input-tax review), beyond noting its accounting-heavy vocabulary.
