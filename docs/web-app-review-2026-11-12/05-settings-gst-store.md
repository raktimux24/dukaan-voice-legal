# 05 · Settings, GST, online store and the set-up-once pages

Reviewer: product design. Scope: Settings hub, Shop profile, Payments & bills, Branding & formats, the GST area (settings/gst/* and products/gst), Opening balances, Billing and Online store & orders. Evidence: screenshots 19–24 and 26 (1440px, top of page only) plus source code. Findings marked "(from code)" come from reading the code, not from a screenshot.

## 1. Verdict

The settings area is carefully built and safe. It has honest copy, owner gating, retry paths and few silent failures. But it is organised around the system's records, not around what a shopkeeper is trying to do. The hub opens on language and text size. Everything a shop owner sets up once is a flat list of nine links on the "Shop" tab, and three of those (Customers, Cashbook, Purchases) are daily work, not settings. Setting up GST and reading GST are in two different places. The GST area has about 15 screens written in compliance and engineering language ("installation identity", "unconfirmed positions", "retained requests"), and none of them shows the one number an owner wants: how much GST is due this month. The online store, which brings in customer orders, can only be reached through Settings or a product page, and nothing tells the owner when a new order arrives.

## 2. Top 5 changes

1. **Restructure Settings around jobs, and move live work out of it.** Make the hub a set of cards: "Getting paid" (UPI, payment methods), "Your bills" (details, logo, layout), "GST", "Your shop", "Team", "Plan", "Your data". Move Online store & orders, Customers, Cashbook and Purchases into the main sidebar. Put the section in the URL so that "← Settings" returns the user to the place they left.
2. **Give GST one home with a money summary at the top.** Combine Payments › GST setup and "GST records & invoice numbers" into one GST page. Its top card should say: "November so far: GST collected ₹X − GST on purchases ₹Y = about ₹Z to pay", with a status chip and one primary button: "Download files for my accountant". Put the technical pages (installations, invoice-number register, providers, rounding, recovery) behind a single "Advanced / for support" link.
3. **Make one "Bill design" page with one live preview.** Today the shop's address and phone are entered in four places: Shop profile, Payments › Bill details, GST legal details and the Online store pickup details. The logo and layout live on a separate Branding page with a second preview. Use the shop profile address everywhere by default ("Use a different address on bills" as an override), and put the header, footer, logo, layout, UPI-on-bill and preview on one screen.
4. **Make orders visible and quick to act on.** Add "Orders" to the sidebar with an unread count. Show new orders on Home and in Alerts. Give each order one clear next-step button. Turn an order into a bill in one step instead of going through Estimate › Convert to bill › Checkout.
5. **Protect the actions that cannot be undone, and say so before the click.** Saving a Regular or Composition GST profile locks the profile, and only support can change it after that. Unpublishing the store takes one click. Deleting the shop or account takes two plain clicks. Add a clear consequence line before each of these, ask for typed confirmation on deletion, and add an "Are you sure?" step for unpublishing.

## 3. Flow walkthrough

### A. "I want customers to pay by UPI and see a QR on the bill"
1. Sidebar › Settings. The page opens on **Preferences** (language, voice, text size). There is nothing about payments here (SAW, 19-settings).
2. The user has to guess that the "02 Shop" tab holds the payment setup. Inside is a two-column list of up to nine links with "↗" arrows, which usually mean "opens a new site". "Payments & bills" is one of them (from code: settings.tsx:425-429, settings.ts:51-135).
3. Payments & bills: a summary strip (Next sale number, Default payment, Cancellation window), then the tabs Payments / Bill details / GST. The UPI form and a live QR preview sit side by side, which works well (SAW, 21-settings-payments).
4. Friction: uploading a QR image **saves straight away**, but typing a UPI ID needs **Save changes**. A hint explains this, but the two ways of saving still conflict (payments.tsx:73). The save bar only appears once the form has changes, and it sits at the bottom of the form, below the fold on desktop (payments.tsx:81).
5. The UPI QR is printed on bills only when a toggle under Branding & formats › Content › "Print configured UPI payment details" is on. That is a different page, two levels deeper (document-branding.tsx:51). Nothing on the Payments page says that this second step exists.

### B. "Put my logo and address on bills"
1. Settings › Shop › Payments & bills › **Bill details** tab: shop name on bill, phone, address, footer, plus a text-only preview whose note says layouts are chosen elsewhere (payments.tsx:78-79).
2. Next is "Branding & formats ↗" › tabs Format / Brand assets / Content. The logo is under "Brand assets". There is a second preview, and a sample generator (document-branding.tsx:45-56).
3. The shop profile also has an address and phone (shop-settings.tsx:69). GST setup asks for a legal address plus an optional "Structured billing address" (gst-ui.tsx:359-377, 448-520). The online store asks for a "Pickup address". The owner cannot tell which of these appears on the bill.

### C. "Turn on GST billing and understand what I owe this month"
1. The obvious door is Settings › Shop › "GST records & invoice numbers". It opens an **integrity dashboard**: Verified documents 9, Records needing review 0, Coverage "Saved records checked" (SAW, 20-settings-gst). There is no rupee figure on the page and no link to GST setup.
2. GST setup actually lives in **Payments & bills › GST** tab. The tab choice is not in the URL, so no link can open that tab directly (payments.tsx:20, 64).
3. The setup form asks for: registration type, effective date, GSTIN and legal name, state, price mode, e-invoice question, and an "I confirm…" checkbox. After saving a Regular or Composition profile, the form locks with "Contact support to change registration or legal details", and nothing warns about this before the save (payment-gst.tsx:15-21).
4. Next, products need tax details. The GST tab lists up to 10 incomplete products as plain text with no links (payment-gst.tsx:25). Bulk review is at /shop/products/gst. Its list cannot be filtered to "missing tax details", and it uses "HSN/SAC", "classification" and "Export GST review file (JSON)" (gst-products.tsx:60-117).
5. "What do I owe?": the only monthly totals are on **GST period review**, which the GST hub does not link to. The path is GST records › GST export history › "More reports & review tools" › GST period review › pick month (gst-reports.tsx:230-240). Even there, sales GST and purchase GST are shown in separate cards as Net / GST / Gross, with no net amount payable (gst-reports.tsx:519-560). That is 6 clicks, and the user still has to do the subtraction.

### D. "Export what my accountant needs"
1. There are three places to export: Settings › Data (Inventory, Activity, Sales CSV for **this month only**, settings.tsx:178-205, 501-531), GST export history (Prepare register: Sales / Purchases / Invoice numbers, each with a date range, gst-reports.tsx:157-215), and GST period review ("preview" CSVs per month).
2. "Prepare register" opens on the **history** tab by default, so a first-time user sees an empty "Saved reports" table and has to switch to "Prepare report" (gst-reports.tsx:59).
3. Nothing produces "one file or zip for October for my CA". The user has to understand what a "register" is and which ones the accountant needs.

### E. "Publish my catalogue and handle an order"
1. The store is reached only from Settings › Shop or from a product page (settings.ts:128; product-detail.tsx:871). It is not in the sidebar, on Home's "shop desk" (SAW, 26-home-lower) or in Alerts.
2. Store tab: "Store is published", the shop name, "Customer catalogue link · samaanbol.space", a full-width "Copy catalogue link" and "▸ View link details" (SAW, 24-online-store). The full link is hidden behind a disclosure, and "Open store" only appears lower down, after the checklist (commerce/view.tsx:271-276).
3. The tab label "Products" wraps to "Produ / cts" at 1440px (SAW, 24-online-store; cause in shop.css:3190 `overflow-wrap:anywhere` on fit-content tabs).
4. Publishing needs Save → Preview → "I reviewed…" checkbox → Publish. **Unpublish is a single click** on the same button, with no confirmation (commerce/view.tsx:256-268).
5. Orders tab: one shared "Reason" text box sits above Accept / Reject / Cancel. All the state buttons have the same visual weight (none is primary). "Mark fulfilled" stays disabled until a sale exists, and nothing next to it explains why (commerce/view.tsx:291-345). "Create bill & collect payment" opens an **Estimate**, and the help text tells the user to "Convert to bill" and then go through checkout (commerce/view.tsx:352-369; retail-locales "commerce.bill_help").

### F. "See my plan"
1. Billing is linked from Settings › Shop as "Subscription" and from the sidebar footer, also labelled "Subscription". The page itself is titled "Billing" (chrome.tsx:191-193; subscription.tsx:16; locale `subscription.section_title` = "Subscription").
2. On the page, the hero element is the shop name in large type. "Premium included for now" appears twice (as a badge and again in a callout), and "Current plan" shows "—" (SAW, 23-billing). A secondary "Refresh status" button is the most prominent control on the page.
3. Cancellation uses a proper `<dialog>` confirmation (AccountClient.tsx:457-461). This works well.

## 5. Not checked

- Anything below the fold in the screenshots: the rest of Preferences, Bill details and GST tabs, Branding, the GST sub-pages, Opening balances, Orders states. All of these were judged from code only.
- Real data states: a shop with GST issues, a shop with open online orders, a Free-plan shop, Manager/Helper views, and offline mode.
- Mobile and tablet widths. The CSS has breakpoints at 760/600px, but no narrow screenshots were provided.
- Keyboard focus order and visible focus outside `.settings-workspace`, screen-reader output, and colour contrast measured with a tool. The contrast comments rely on hex values read from the CSS.
- AccountDashboard (Billing) beyond the top of the page and the cancel dialog: the plan switch, payment history and the Razorpay checkout.
- Whether the server actually blocks a GST profile change after it is locked, or whether only the UI does.
- The content of the publicly published catalogue page that customers see.
