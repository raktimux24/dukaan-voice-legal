# Selling, end to end: design review

Scope: Sell (bill building), Checkout and payment, bill page after the sale (share, print), Sales register, Returns and voids, Estimates.
Sources: screenshots `02-sell.jpg`, `03-sales.jpg`, `16-estimates.jpg` (1440px, top of page only), plus code under `app/components/shop/` and `app/lib/shop/`. Findings marked "(from code)" were not seen in a screenshot.

## 1. Verdict

The selling core is solid. The search box takes typing and barcodes, Enter adds the top match, weight items open a quantity sheet, and the shortest path from an empty bill to a recorded cash sale is about 4 clicks plus typing. The flow is let down by what surrounds that core. The Sell page shows a mode switch, three scanner entry points and a "Draft & recovery" panel, and the search box does not have focus when the page opens. Checkout has a tax-loading state that looks like an error. After the sale, the bill page reads like an audit record, not a receipt. Network and safety rules are applied in ways that block the counter: with one unconfirmed bill the whole Sell page locks, and a product the system thinks is out of stock cannot be sold. Copy for recovery, returns and estimates is written for accountants, not shopkeepers. Most of the fixes are about focus, defaults and removing steps, not a rebuild.

## 2. Top 5 changes

1. **Keep the counter open when the network fails.** Today one unconfirmed bill locks the search box and every add button until it is resolved (sell.tsx:123, 357). Park it as "1 bill waiting to sync" and let the cashier start the next bill.
2. **Make the search box the scanner, with focus always on it.** Autofocus search when the page opens and after every action, drop the separate "USB scanner" field and the "Sell / Add stock" switch from the bill, and flash the line that was just added (sell.tsx:367, 374-383).
3. **Never block a sale at the counter because recorded stock says 0.** Let the cashier override with a one-tap "Sell anyway" that records the shortfall, which the system already handles at checkout (sell.tsx:211-214, 287).
4. **Turn the bill page into a success screen.** Show a large "Paid ₹X" with Share on WhatsApp / Print / New sale (Enter) as the first and only primary actions. Move provenance, cost and margin, recovery and management below a fold (sale-detail.tsx:298-416).
5. **Show checkout loading as loading.** While tax details load, show a skeleton amount, not "Unavailable" with a red alert. Expand item review by default for bills with 5 items or fewer, and add a "₹1,000" chip and round-up chips for cash (checkout.tsx:44, 149, 311, 342, 353).

## 3. Flow walkthrough

Scenario: a shopkeeper at a laptop with a USB barcode gun, a customer waiting, 3 items (one sold by weight), a returning customer, cash payment, a WhatsApp bill. Later, the customer returns one item.

## 5. Not checked

- Anything below the fold or in interaction states: no screenshots exist of a bill with items, search results, the quantity sheet, checkout, UPI QR, split, udhaar, the bill page, returns, the estimate editor or the estimate detail. All of those findings come from code.
- Real timing: how long the catalogue, tax snapshot and rounding queries take on slow networks, or whether the Sell page feels slow in practice.
- Actual offline behaviour of GST-registered shops that use pre-provisioned offline invoice numbers (`prepareOfflineInvoices`, `LocalFiscalReceiptScreen`). S01 applies at least to shops without that path.
- Barcode-gun behaviour across real hardware (prefix/suffix characters, speed), camera scanning quality, and the Web Share API on Windows/Mac browsers.
- Print output: the receipt layout on A4 versus thermal printers, and the branded-document PDF quality.
- Colour contrast measured with a tool. The values cited are read from CSS and look adequate in contrast but small in size.
- Screen-reader behaviour of the sheets (`VoiceSheet`) and the `<dialog>` used by estimates, and the focus return after closing them.
- Responsive layouts below 1440px. CSS has tablet and phone rules, but none were viewed.
- The voice-sell path (`/shop/voice?mode=sell`), the GST adjustment and debit-note screens beyond a code skim, and translated (Hindi, Tamil and other) string lengths in the selling UI.
