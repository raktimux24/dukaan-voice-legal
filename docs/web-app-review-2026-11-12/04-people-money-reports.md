# People, money and insight: design review (2026-11-12)

Scope: Customers, customer detail, udhaar repayment, statements and credit, payment follow-ups, customer import, Staff, Expenses & cashbook (count and close day), Reports (sales and stock), Activity.
Sources: screenshots 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 (1440px, top of page only) and code under `app/components/shop/` and `app/lib/shop/{cashbook,followups,credit}/`. Items marked "(from code)" were not seen on screen.

## 1. Verdict

The data underneath is strong. Balances, ledgers, the cashbook audit trail and the follow-up history are careful and safe. The screens show that care too directly, though. The main jobs (take a repayment, close the till, chase the biggest debtor) are buried behind disclosures, tabs, required notes and legal-sounding copy that reads like an accountant's checklist, not like something for a shopkeeper at 9pm. The visual shell is calm and premium: big numbers, a quiet dark theme, a clear saffron accent. What's missing is a clear "do this next" action on each screen. Several first views are also empty or contradictory: follow-ups shows "No matching customers" while 14 people owe money, Reports opens on a ₹0 "Today" with a blank chart, and the export button says "Premium" next to Premium-only data. Fixing the five items below would do more for "easy" than any visual polish.

## 2. Top 5 changes

1. **Make "Receive payment" the main action on customer detail and on each owing row.** Show it as an open panel (amount pre-filled with the full balance, then Cash/UPI chips and a "New balance after this payment" line), not as a closed `<details>`. Show a clear success state with a WhatsApp receipt option.
2. **Rebuild follow-ups around "who owes the most".** Default to "All with udhaar, highest first", so the queue is never empty while money is owed. Let a reminder go out for the whole balance without agreed due dates, and drop the required notes on "Mark as sent", snooze and settings.
3. **Turn Count & close day into a guided 3-step close.** The steps: expected cash, then counted cash (with an optional note-by-note counter), then a coloured "₹X short / ₹X extra / Matches" result. Allow managers to close, and make the step the primary action after 6pm. Put the cashbook in the sidebar.
4. **Open Reports on a useful view.** Default to "This week" (or Today only when there are sales today). Use period chips instead of a dropdown, put "Top sellers" above the fold, show a real empty state instead of a blank chart, and fix the "Export CSV · Premium" label.
5. **Rewrite the copy in plain shop language across cashbook, follow-ups, credit and activity.** For example, "Retain count & close day" becomes "Close today", "Owner cash movements" becomes "Cash in / out", "Cash count retained" becomes "Day closed – cash counted", "obligation/allocation/exposure" becomes "bill / match payment to bill / total udhaar". Move the legal caveats into a single "How this works" link.

## 3. Flow walkthrough

### A. A regular pays back part of their udhaar
1. Sidebar, then **Customers** (screen 10). There are three big summary numbers, then a full-width search field. Friction: the search field has no placeholder or icon and sits below the tabs (SAW). Search fires a request on every keystroke, with no debounce (from code, `customers.tsx:15`).
2. Type a name, then open the row. The whole row is a link, which is good. Friction: rows have no inline "Receive" action, so every repayment costs an extra page load.
3. **Customer detail** (from code, `customer-detail.tsx:30-33`). It shows the balance, purchases this month and last purchase, then a risk hint. "Record payment ＋" is a **collapsed** `<details>`. Friction: the most common action on this page is hidden behind a click, and it looks the same as the secondary disclosures.
4. Expand the panel. You get an Amount field, a "Full" button, a Method `<select>` (Cash/UPI/**Card**), a Note field, and "Match this payment to bills (optional)" with long help text. Friction: there's no live "balance after" preview. Nothing explains what happens if the amount is more than the balance (it becomes an advance). "Full" is a cryptic label. Card shows here even though card is hidden elsewhere in POS (`gst-customer-payment.tsx:41-44`).
5. Submit. The fields clear and the queries refresh. Friction: there's **no success message** or receipt offer. `useGstAction` only renders a notice on error (`gst-workspace.tsx:114-139`), so the only sign of success is the balance number changing above the fold.

### B. 9pm: close the day and check the cash matches
1. Find the cashbook. It **isn't in the sidebar**. You reach it from Home ("Money & people"), from a link at the bottom of Reports, or from Settings (from code: `chrome.tsx:60-90`, `home.tsx:450`, `reports.tsx:397`). The page's back link says "← Reports", and no sidebar item is highlighted (SAW, 15).
2. **Daily cashbook** (screen 15). The large "Expected closing cash" figure is good. Friction: the date field shows `12/11/2026` (ambiguous order) while the help text says `6/11/2026, 12:00:00 am`, with seconds and a different format (SAW). The "Daily cashbook" tab label wraps onto two lines at 1440px (SAW).
3. Action row: **Add expense** (primary), Owner cash movements, **Count & close day**, Share/download CSV (SAW). Friction: closing the day has the same weight as CSV export. The four buttons are a mix of daily and rare tasks. Only the **owner** sees Count & close (`cashbook/view.tsx:783-797`), so a manager closing at 9pm cannot.
4. Count form (from code, `view.tsx:657-705`): heading, a long help paragraph, a "Expected closing cash: ₹X" text line, one "Cash counted in the till (₹)" field, then a plain text "Difference: ₹-120.00". Friction: there's no plus/minus meaning in words (short vs extra), no colour, and no help counting notes. The required checkbox, "I checked all counters and pending bills…", adds a step. The button says "Retain count & close day".
5. Result (`view.tsx:808-820`): a "Retained cash count" card with expected / counted / difference. Friction: there's no closing summary to share (for example to the owner on WhatsApp), and no "see you tomorrow" sense of being done.

### C. See who owes the most and send a reminder
1. Customers, then **Open balances** tab (SAW, 10). There's no sort control. Whether the list is ordered by amount owed isn't stated.
2. "Payment follow-ups ↗" (quiet link, top right). **Follow-ups** (screen 11) opens on "Due today or earlier" and shows **"No matching customers on this page."** with a "Show more customers" button below the empty state (SAW). That's while the Customers page says 14 people owe ₹75,591.50. Friction: the queue only counts bills with an *agreed due date* (`followups.unknown` copy, `followups/view.tsx:11,22`). Most udhaar in a kirana shop has no due date, so the default view is empty.
3. Open a customer's follow-up (from code). You have to choose due-dated bills, choose a language, then "Preview reminder". The preview shows long caveats. Then WhatsApp/SMS/Copy, then a required "Reason / contact reference" note before **Mark as sent** (`view.tsx:23`). Snooze and settings each need a required note too (`view.tsx:18,24`). Friction: there are about 6 steps and 2 typed justifications to send one WhatsApp message.

### D. Invite a helper
1. Sidebar, then **Staff** (screen 12). The back link says "← Settings", which doesn't match the sidebar's "People" group (SAW). The role counters (Owner 1 / Manager 0 / Helper 0) take a full band of space (SAW).
2. "Invite teammate +" opens a sheet with a Role select (defaults to Helper, which is good), a role description, the invite code, then Share invite / Copy code / Copy app link (`staff.tsx:105`). This is the smoothest flow in the area. Friction: there's no WhatsApp button (on desktop `navigator.share` often falls back to clipboard). Pending invites aren't shown, so you can't see whether the helper joined. The link opens the *phone* app, which may confuse a laptop user.
3. Member detail is in-page state, not a URL. The browser Back button leaves Staff instead of returning to the list (from code, `staff.tsx:25,91`).

### E. Check what sold best this week
1. Sidebar, then **Reports** (screen 13). It opens on **Today**: Revenue ₹0.00, 0 bills, and a blank "Sales by hour" panel with no empty message (SAW).
2. Change the "Report period" dropdown to 7 days. Friction: this is a select, not one-tap chips.
3. Scroll past Revenue tiles, the by-day chart and the payment mix to reach **Top products** (`reports.tsx:920`). The Quantity/Revenue toggle is good. Friction: "best this week" sits below the fold, and quantity ranks products sold in different units (kg vs pcs) against each other.

## 5. Not checked

- Anything below the fold in a live session: customer detail, the statement PDF preview, the follow-up preview, the count-and-close form, the expenses list, the stock report, and the Ask box were judged from code only.
- Real behaviour of server sorting (customer list order, follow-up queue order) and whether the screen-13 entitlement mismatch is a timing artefact.
- Narrow widths (tablet and phone) and the sidebar collapse. I only had 1440px screenshots, so I read the CSS media queries but didn't see them render.
- Keyboard order, screen-reader announcements and focus return after the sheets (VoiceSheet) close. A global `:focus-visible` outline exists (`shop.css:80`), but I didn't exercise it.
- Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil and Telugu rendering, and copy length in those languages (several labels already wrap in English).
- Helper and Manager views: I only judged them from code branches, and screenshots are Owner only.
- Performance and timing of the PDF export (`html-to-image` and `pdf-lib` run in the browser).
